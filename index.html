The Case of the Missing Chimpanzee

Story created by Jaylyn, Reiko, Yeezhen and Yi Bin

Reiko was getting ready for a sleepover with Gerald when she noticed that Teo Yibin, her plush chimpanzee, was missing from his toy bed. His little fabric banana was gone too.

Someone had set up a surprise treasure hunt. Reiko asked Detective Logico to work out who moved her toy, what they carried it in and where they left it.

Suspects

  • Gerald is Reiko’s best friend and sleepover guest.
  • Tan Yeezhen is Reiko’s dad. He said he had been playing digital mahjong.

Objects & Locations

Objects

  • Blue basket
  • Snack tray

Locations

  • Kitchen
  • Pantry

Evidence

At 7.05 p.m., Gerald and Tan Yeezhen were in different rooms. One had the blue basket and the other had the snack tray. Only one of them moved Teo Yibin.

  1. A photo taken at 7.05 p.m. shows Gerald in the kitchen carrying the snack tray.
  2. The mahjong game record shows that Tan Yeezhen’s round ended at 7.00 p.m., five minutes before Teo Yibin was moved.
  3. A pantry photo taken at 7.05 p.m. shows someone setting a blue basket on the shelf beside the mini fridge. The person’s face is out of frame, but Teo Yibin and his fabric banana are visible inside the basket.
  4. A note on the basket reads, “You found the last stop of the sleepover treasure hunt!”

Teacher Solution

Tan Yeezhen moved Teo Yibin in the blue basket and left him in the pantry.

Both photos were taken at 7.05 p.m. Gerald was in the kitchen with the snack tray, so Tan Yeezhen was the person in the pantry with the blue basket. His mahjong round had already ended. The note explains why he moved the toy.
